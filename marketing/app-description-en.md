



KRAINA is a mobile app for discovering historical places, routes, and social travel experiences.



KRAINA helps users explore cities through historical content, routes, and modern digital experiences.

Main features:

- user registration and personal profile;
- discovery of historical locations;
- route creation and saving;
- social features (follow system, feed, interaction);
- multilingual interface.

KRAINA is built for people who want to combine travel, learning, and technology in one app.
